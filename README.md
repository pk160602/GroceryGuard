# 🛒 GroceryGuard

### Smart Inventory. Less Waste. Better Business.

GroceryGuard is a grocery shop management web application designed to help small grocery shopkeepers digitally manage their inventory, products, sales, customers, and payments.

The project was developed to address common manual problems faced by local grocery shopkeepers, such as incorrect stock records, difficulty tracking product expiry dates, customer record management, and payment tracking.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Small grocery shops often depend on notebooks, memory, and manual calculations to manage their daily operations.

GroceryGuard provides a simple digital solution that allows a shopkeeper to:

- Manage products and inventory
- Track stock quantities
- Monitor low-stock products
- Track product expiry dates
- Record sales
- Manage customer records
- Track customer payments and credit
- View important information from a central dashboard

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Manual grocery shop management can result in:

- Overstocking of products
- Products getting expired
- Incorrect inventory records
- Difficulty finding customer information
- Errors in sales calculations
- Difficulty tracking credit and payments
- Time-consuming manual record keeping

GroceryGuard aims to reduce these problems by providing a centralized digital management system.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 📊 Dashboard

Provides a quick overview of the shop, including:

- Today's sales
- Total products
- Available stock
- Low-stock products
- Expiring products
- Expired products

### 📦 Inventory Management

- Add products
- Edit products
- Delete products
- View current stock
- Store purchase price
- Store selling price
- Store supplier information
- Track expiry dates
- Set minimum stock levels

### 🧾 Sales Management

- Record product sales
- Select products and quantities
- Calculate sale totals
- Select payment method
- Update inventory after sales

### 👥 Customer Management

- Add customers
- Store customer information
- View customer records
- Associate customers with sales

### 💰 Payment Tracking

- Record customer payments
- Track credit transactions
- Store payment method
- Store payment references
- Maintain payment history
